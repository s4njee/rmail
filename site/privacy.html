Privacy policy

Effective September 18, 2026

In short: Quill Mail ("Quill") is a desktop app that runs on your computer. It connects directly to your email and calendar providers and stores what it downloads only on your computer. The people who make Quill run no servers that receive your mail, your contacts, your calendar, or your sign-in details, and cannot see them.

What Quill accesses

When you add an account, Quill signs in to that provider with your permission and accesses:

  • Email: messages, attachments, folders or labels, and message status such as read, starred, or archived, so it can show, search, organize, and send your mail.
  • Calendar (Google and CalDAV accounts): your calendars and events, so it can show them and save the changes you make.
  • Your account's email address and name, to label the account and address the mail you send.

Quill only takes actions you ask for, such as sending a message, moving mail, or editing an event, plus the rules you set up yourself.

Where your data is stored

  • Mail, attachments, calendar events, search indexes, and settings are stored in Quill's data folder on your computer.
  • Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored in your operating system's secure credential store (the macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, or the Linux Secret Service). They are never written into Quill's database, logs, or backups.
  • Quill's log files stay on your computer. They don't include message content, email addresses, or credentials.

What leaves your computer

Quill connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Your mail and calendar providers, over encrypted connections, to sync and send. This is the core of the app.
  • Google or Microsoft sign-in, when you add or reconnect an account that uses browser sign-in. The sign-in happens in your web browser and the result goes straight to Quill on your computer.
  • Account setup lookups. To find your server settings, Quill looks up your email domain (for example example.com, never your full address) in DNS, on your provider's domain, and in Mozilla's public mail-settings database at autoconfig.thunderbird.net.
  • Remote content you choose to load. Images and other remote content in messages are blocked by default. If you load them for a message or trust a sender, your computer fetches them from the sender's servers, as any mail app would.
  • Unsubscribe requests, only when you click Unsubscribe on a message. They go to the address the sender published for that purpose.
  • Update checks, to Quill's release page on GitHub. They share only what any web request does, such as your IP address.
  • Crash reports and a usage ping, only if you turn them on. Both are off by default (Settings → Diagnostics). A crash report contains the app version, operating system, and a scrubbed error message and stack trace. It never includes message content, email addresses, folder names, or credentials. The usage ping is one message per launch containing only the app version, operating system, and processor type, with no identifier of any kind.

Quill does not sell your data, show ads, share your data with third parties, or use your mail or calendar to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Google user data

If you connect a Google account, Quill requests access to Gmail (https://mail.google.com/) to read, organize, and send your mail over IMAP and SMTP, to Google Calendar to show and edit your events, and to your basic profile and email address to identify the account.

Quill's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google user data:

  • is used only to provide the mail and calendar features you use in Quill;
  • is stored only on your computer and is not transferred to Quill's developers or anyone else;
  • is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI or machine learning models;
  • is never seen by Quill's developers or any other person, because Quill sends it nowhere except back to Google.

Microsoft accounts

If you connect a Microsoft account, Quill requests access to your mail over IMAP and SMTP, your email address and basic profile, and offline access so it can keep syncing without asking you to sign in each time. That data is handled exactly as described above.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Remove an account in Settings → Accounts. This deletes the account's downloaded mail, attachments, and calendar data, and its stored credentials, from your computer. Nothing is deleted from your provider.
  • Uninstall Quill and delete its data folder to remove everything it stored.
  • Revoke access at any time from your provider: Google account permissions or Microsoft app access.

Children

Quill is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. The full history is public in the project's repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/s4njee/rmail/issues.
