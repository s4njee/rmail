Your mail, fast and on your own computer.

Quill is a desktop mail and calendar client for Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail, and any IMAP account. It has no servers of its own: your mail travels straight between your computer and your provider.

A calm, quick place to do your mail

A native app is faster than a browser tab and a lot lighter than a traditional desktop suite.

Every account, one inbox

Gmail, Microsoft 365, iCloud, Fastmail, and standard IMAP side by side, with replies sent from the address the mail came to.

Works offline

Read, search, archive, and write without a connection. Changes queue up and sync when you're back online, and you can undo them.

Private by default

Remote images, including tracking pixels, stay blocked until you allow them, per message or per sender. Links show where they really go before they open.

Instant search

Search runs on your computer, with operators like from:, has:attachment, and before:, plus saved searches.

Keyboard first

j k to move, e to archive, r to reply, / to search. Press ? for the rest.

Snooze, send later, undo send

Push mail to later, schedule replies, and take back a message in the seconds after you hit Send.

How your data flows

Quill signs in to your provider and syncs your mail and calendar to your computer. Nothing passes through the Quill developers.

  • Google accounts. With your permission, Quill uses Gmail's IMAP and SMTP access to show, organize, and send your mail, and Google Calendar to show and edit your events. That data is stored only on your computer.
  • Microsoft accounts. Quill uses Outlook's IMAP and SMTP access to show, organize, and send your mail, stored the same way.
  • Your sign-in. Passwords and sign-in tokens are kept in your operating system's keychain, never in Quill's files or backups.
  • No ads, no selling, no training. Your mail is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train AI models. The privacy policy has the details.
